Website Development → Custom Web Application

Built around your business, not a one-size-fits-all website.

A custom-built digital application designed around your business requirements, not a one-size-fits-all website.

Website vs Custom Web Application

A website — including our Premium tier — presents information and content to visitors. Even with forms and advanced content management, its core job is communicating.

A Custom Web Application is software people use to do something — register, log in, manage orders, track data, complete a workflow. It stores and processes data, not just displays it.

What This Can Include

Depending on what your business needs.

→

User registration & secure login

Different people accessing different parts of the system securely.

→

Customer-facing application

A space for your customers to interact directly with your business.

→

Administrative dashboard

A clear view of what's happening in the business at a glance.

→

Database & information management

Real, structured storage and management of your business's records.

→

User roles & permissions

Control over who can see and do what within the system.

→

Customer workflows

Structured processes — scheduling, approvals, multi-step tasks.

→

Product / service order management

Handle orders and requests through the system itself.

→

Lead & customer data collection

Capture and manage the information your business runs on.

→

Payment or third-party integration

Connected to the tools and services your business needs, where required.

These are examples of what a Custom Web Application can include — not a list of finished products. What your project actually needs is scoped individually.

Suitable for

  • E-commerce platforms
  • Booking systems
  • Loan platforms
  • Customer portals
  • Other custom business solutions

Pricing
