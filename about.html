About NCBiz Digital Consults

A digital consultancy built around real business objectives.

Build, Optimize, Grow.

NCBiz Digital Consults is a Ugandan digital consultancy and technology solutions company. We help businesses build professional digital presences, improve their visibility on Google, market themselves effectively online, and use technology to operate more efficiently.

Who We Are

A Ugandan digital consultancy and technology company.

NCBiz Digital Consults is based in Kampala, Uganda. We help businesses improve their digital presence, become easier to find online and use technology to run more effectively — whether that starts with a first website, better Google visibility, a marketing plan or a system built around a specific business process.

We work with small businesses, startups and established companies. Our starting point is always the business itself: what it needs to achieve, who its customers are and where technology can genuinely help.

Our Journey

From compliance and digital services to digital consultancy.

NCBiz began with a focus on business compliance and digital services. As our founder researched digital technology and observed the challenges businesses face, the company’s focus shifted more strongly towards digital consultancy, digital growth and technology solutions.

Business compliance & digital services → Digital consultancy & growth → Technology solutions

The motivation is practical: helping businesses understand and use websites, search visibility, digital marketing and business systems that fit the way they work. We also want useful technology to be more accessible to Ugandan businesses, including through locally relevant solutions and pricing.

We are a growing company and we would rather say so plainly. What we bring is continuous learning, practical advice and solutions designed around the business in front of us.

Most recently, NCBiz Digital Consults was nominated for Outstanding IT Company of the Year at the National Quality Awards 2026. Read the nomination announcement.

Our Approach

Business-first, not template-first.

→

Business First, Not Template First

Every project starts with what the business needs to achieve — who its customers are, how they find it and what should happen next. Pages and features follow from that, not from a generic checklist or a stock template.

→

Practical Technology

We choose tools and approaches that fit the business and its budget, not whatever is trendiest. Most customers will reach a business through their phone, so mobile experience is where design starts.

→

Search Visibility

Google visibility is planned into the build from day one — page structure, useful content and technical basics — rather than added afterwards. We can’t promise rankings, and we’re cautious of anyone who does.

→

Build for the Business

Custom systems exist to solve a specific operational problem, not to look impressive. The starting question is always “what should this achieve for the business?” before any design or development begins.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our work.

These are the principles we try to work by as a growing company — how we advise, what we build and how we treat the businesses we work with.

→

Purposeful Innovation

We recommend and build technology because it solves a defined problem, not because it is new.

→

Client-Centred Thinking

We start by understanding the business, its customers and its constraints, then shape the solution around them.

→

Practical Accessibility

We want useful digital tools within reach of Ugandan businesses — explained in plain language, and designed and priced with the local market in mind.

→

Continuous Learning

Search, marketing and technology keep changing, so we keep researching and testing to make sure our advice stays useful.

Integrity and Accountability
