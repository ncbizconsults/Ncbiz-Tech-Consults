NCBiz Digital Consults Nominated for Outstanding IT Company of the Year — National Quality Awards 2026

Our nomination for Outstanding IT Company of the Year, communicated by NETA-Uganda — and what we’re building towards.

Published 25 October 2026 · NCBiz Digital Consults

Nomination announcement

NCBiz Digital Consults has been nominated in the Outstanding IT Company of the Year category at the National Quality Awards 2026.

The nomination, communicated by the National Entrepreneurs and Traders Association of Uganda (NETA-Uganda), is an important moment for our growing digital consultancy and technology solutions company based in Kampala, Uganda.

Our work focuses on helping businesses strengthen their digital presence, improve their visibility online and explore practical technology solutions that support the way they operate and grow.

We welcome this recognition as an opportunity to share our journey, the problems we are working to solve and our vision for making useful digital solutions more accessible to businesses.

About the nomination

The nomination places NCBiz Digital Consults in the Outstanding IT Company of the Year category at the National Quality Awards 2026.

According to the nomination letter, the awards event is scheduled for 27 November 2026 at 5:00 PM at Kampala Marriott Hotel.

We are grateful to NETA-Uganda for communicating this nomination and for the opportunity to share more about our work.

A nomination is not an award. It does not guarantee a win, and it is not a certification or endorsement of our services beyond what the nomination letter itself states.

Our journey

NCBiz Digital Consults began with a focus that included business compliance and digital services. As we learned more about the challenges businesses face, our direction evolved.

We saw opportunities to help businesses not only establish an online presence, but also become easier to find, communicate more effectively with customers and use technology to manage everyday work.

That journey led us to focus more deeply on digital consultancy, online visibility, digital marketing, website development and technology solutions.

Our direction is shaped by continuous learning, research and a desire to understand how digital tools can solve practical problems in our own market. We want businesses to have somewhere they can turn for guidance — whether they need a better website, improved Google visibility, a digital marketing strategy or a system designed around a particular business process. You can read more on our About page.

What we do

We combine digital consultancy with implementation, helping businesses identify what they need and work towards a practical solution.

Our work includes:

  • Website design and development
  • SEO and Google visibility
  • Digital marketing and online campaigns
  • Web applications and business systems
  • Digital strategy and consultancy

We believe that digital growth is not just about being online. It is about using the right tools for the right reasons.

Why the nomination matters

As a growing Ugandan company, we see this nomination as an encouraging moment in our journey. It gives us an opportunity to introduce our work to more people and explain the purpose behind the services and technology we are building.

Our ambition is to help more businesses understand and benefit from digital tools — not only through websites and marketing, but also through better systems and thoughtful technology choices.

We recognise that building a useful technology company takes time, learning, collaboration and consistent work. We remain focused on developing solutions that serve real needs and creating meaningful value for the businesses we work with.

Thank you to NETA-Uganda

We thank the National Entrepreneurs and Traders Association of Uganda (NETA-Uganda) for communicating our nomination for Outstanding IT Company of the Year at the National Quality Awards 2026.

We also appreciate the clients, supporters and people who have encouraged our work and trusted us with opportunities to learn, build and improve. Every project and conversation helps us better understand the challenges businesses face and the solutions that may help them move forward.
