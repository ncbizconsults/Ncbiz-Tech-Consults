NCBiz Digital Consults Earns TechBehemoths Awards 2026 Nomination Across Three Technology Categories

The Ugandan digital consultancy and technology company reaches an international milestone following its nomination by Berlin-based technology platform TechBehemoths.

Published 17 November 2026 · NCBiz Digital Consults

KAMPALA, UGANDA — NOVEMBER 2026

NCBiz Digital Consults has been officially nominated for the TechBehemoths Awards 2026 in three technology categories: E-Commerce Development, Mobile App Development, and ReactJS.

The nomination marks an important milestone for the Ugandan digital consultancy and technology company, placing its work among businesses recognised through an international technology platform.

For NCBiz Digital Consults, this is more than a nomination. It represents a meaningful step in the company's journey of building digital solutions, supporting business growth, and contributing to the evolving technology landscape.

Vote for NCBiz Digital Consults in the TechBehemoths Awards 2026

From a Business Vision to Digital Solutions

NCBiz Digital Consults began with a vision to help businesses address practical challenges and take advantage of opportunities in the digital space.

What started as an ambition to support businesses has developed into a broader digital consultancy and technology operation. Its work has grown to include digital visibility, search engine optimisation, website development, e-commerce solutions, digital marketing, and technology-driven business solutions.

The company's approach is built around understanding business needs first, then applying the right digital strategy and technology to help businesses build, optimise, and grow.

This business-first philosophy continues to shape the services and products developed under the NCBiz name.

Building Technology That Addresses Real Business Needs

Beyond providing digital consultancy services, NCBiz Digital Consults is also developing technology products designed to address practical operational challenges.

Two of these products reflect the company's growing focus on building solutions for businesses.

BizRght — Every Transaction. Done Right.

BizRght is an accounting and business management platform designed with the needs of businesses in mind, particularly within the Ugandan market.

The platform focuses on helping businesses manage transactions, invoices, receipts, and business records through a digital workspace.

Its goal is to make everyday business record-keeping more organised and accessible.

Explore BizRght →

ServeLane — Know Your Orders. Grow Your Business.

ServeLane is a restaurant operations and digital ordering platform designed to help restaurants manage important parts of their daily operations.

It brings together functions such as digital menus, order management, and kitchen workflows, helping restaurant teams organise orders and serve customers more efficiently.

Explore ServeLane →

Together, BizRght and ServeLane demonstrate NCBiz Digital Consults' ambition to move beyond providing digital services and develop practical technology products for businesses.

An International Milestone for a Ugandan Technology Company

The TechBehemoths Awards nomination places NCBiz Digital Consults on an international platform alongside companies operating in the technology sector.

Being nominated across E-Commerce Development, Mobile App Development, and ReactJS is an encouraging milestone for a company building its presence from Uganda.

It also reinforces the importance of creating technology solutions that respond to real business needs while developing the technical capabilities required to compete in a wider digital environment.

NCBiz Digital Consults views this nomination as motivation to keep improving its services, strengthening its products, and pursuing meaningful innovation.

Support NCBiz Digital Consults in the TechBehemoths Awards 2026

NCBiz Digital Consults is inviting clients, partners, colleagues, supporters, and the wider community to participate in the voting process.

One vote supports NCBiz Digital Consults across its three nominated service categories.
