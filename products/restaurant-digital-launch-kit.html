QR / Table Ordering

Scan, order, done.

A customer scans the QR code at their table, browses the menu on their own phone, builds their order, and sends it straight to the restaurant on WhatsApp — no app, no waiting for a waiter to take the order.

The customer journey: Scan QR → identify table → browse menu → select food → review cart → submit order.

Table identified — the customer's table number is captured automatically from the QR code.
Review order — the customer confirms items, quantities and the total before sending.
Send via WhatsApp — the order is handed off to WhatsApp, ready for the customer to send.
Order confirmed — with a natural prompt to leave a Google review after the meal, supporting the restaurant's Google visibility.

What's Included

Everything a restaurant needs to start.

✓ A real restaurant website — homepage, menu, about, and contact/location information

✓ A digital menu customers can browse on their own phones

✓ QR/table ordering with automatic table identification

✓ Food selection, cart review, and order submission

✓ WhatsApp Business ordering workflow — orders sent directly to the restaurant's WhatsApp

✓ A natural Google Review prompt after ordering, supporting local visibility

✓ Google Business Profile / local visibility support, so the restaurant is easier to find on Google Search and Maps

The Launch Kit doesn't include an online payment gateway — ordering and payment currently work through WhatsApp and in-person payment at the restaurant.
