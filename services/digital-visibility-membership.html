The Problem

Being online is not the same as being found, or trusted.

A website that isn’t being found

A website can exist and still be hard to discover when people search for what you offer.

Information that is scattered or inconsistent

Business details spread across listings and profiles can be incomplete, out of date or inconsistent.

Little for customers to check

Before they trust a business, many customers look for its LinkedIn page, listings and third-party mentions, not just its website.

No one owning the ongoing work

Digital visibility is not a one-time project. It needs steady attention, planning and review.

Who it is designed for: businesses and organisations that want their website, search visibility, business listings, LinkedIn presence and digital credibility managed together over a full year, with clear reporting, rather than commissioning each piece separately.

What the Membership Covers

Five pillars, one structured programme.

01

Website, SEO & Content Management

Routine website maintenance, SEO improvements and an agreed allowance of website content, managed as part of the programme.

02

Business Discovery & Listing Management

Helping customers find your business, and find accurate information about it, across relevant business listings.

03

LinkedIn Company Authority Management

Management of your LinkedIn company page, as described in the agreed membership scope.

AI search visibility optimisation, alongside management of selected Google and LinkedIn advertising campaigns. Advertising budgets are funded separately.

Bringing these activities under one programme means they are planned together, reported on together and reviewed together. Your website content, listings, LinkedIn presence and outreach can support one another, instead of being separate tasks handled in isolation.

How the 12-Month Programme Works

From first conversation to quarterly review.

  1. Free initial consultation. We discuss your business, your objectives and which option fits.
  2. Individual proposal and service schedule. The detail of what is included for you is documented in writing, not left to this public page.
  3. Agreement and onboarding. Once an agreement is signed, onboarding and access arrangements are handled separately.
  4. Ongoing management. NCBiz manages the agreed work across the five pillars through the 12-month term.
  5. Monthly performance reporting. You receive regular reports on the work and its progress.
  6. Quarterly strategic review meetings. We review progress with you and plan the next stage.
